# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People in and around Dallas, Pennsylvania who are shopping for a used vehicle and need to quickly browse real inventory, understand the next step, and contact a nearby dealership. The site also serves returning local customers looking to call, get directions, ask about financing, or discuss a trade-in.

## Product Purpose

Rick's Used Cars is a local independent auto dealership site. It makes the dealership's current vehicle inventory, contact routes, financing inquiry, trade-in inquiry, delivery information, warranty information, and reviews available online while preserving the dealer's existing lead workflow.

## Positioning

The site represents a real, walk-in local dealership at 601 Main Rd in Dallas, Pennsylvania, rather than an abstract vehicle marketplace. It should make calling, getting directions, and discussing a specific vehicle feel immediate.

## Operating Context

Visitors are commonly on a phone, browsing listings between errands or comparing vehicles from home. The live product has an inventory API with filtering, sorting, pagination, detail galleries, lead forms, contact-action tracking, and a local PostgreSQL-backed FastAPI service.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing FastAPI endpoints, data structures, vehicle filtering and pagination, lead submission, validation, routing, analytics, integrations, and working business logic.
- Redesign all customer-facing React pages and shared UI without retaining the incumbent visual language.
- Confirmed public details: Rick's Used Cars; 601 Main Rd, Dallas, PA 18612; +1 (570) 675-6029.
- Google Maps shows the business as an Auto Dealer with a 4.8 rating from 64 reviews and a wheelchair-accessible entrance at the time of review.
- No official website was listed in the supplied Google Maps card. Full weekly hours remain unconfirmed and must not be invented.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Rick's Used Cars.
- Present a mature, straightforward, trustworthy, local Pennsylvania automotive identity with a slightly rugged character.
- Avoid luxury-startup, generic SaaS, and AI-template aesthetics.

## Evidence on Hand

- Google Maps place record supplied by the user; confirmed contact details and physical context are recorded above.
- Existing app inventory API and real vehicle data.
- Existing public pages, forms, and routing are functional source material, but inherited visual assets and copy are not brand authority for Rick's.

## Product Principles

- Let real vehicles and clear next steps do the selling.
- Make the local dealership easy to call and find from every screen.
- Keep browsing dense, legible, and practical on mobile as well as desktop.
- Preserve functioning commerce and lead flows while replacing the visual world completely.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Use semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus indicators, useful image alternatives, and sufficient contrast.
- Treat mobile as a first-class experience and avoid horizontal overflow at common phone widths.
