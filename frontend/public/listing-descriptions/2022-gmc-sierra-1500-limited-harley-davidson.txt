This 2022 GMC Sierra 1500 Limited SLT Crew Cab is an Official Harley-Davidson example produced by Tuscany Motors of Elkhart, Indiana. It is finished in Jet Black over black leather and features 22” Harley-Davidson wheels, a lifted suspension, power-retractable running boards, and a unique front grille, hood, fender vents, and bumpers. The cabin is appointed with a Harley-Davidson-branded instrument cluster, center console, door-sill plates, and pedals as well as heated and ventilated front seats, a Bose sound system, and a touchscreen infotainment system. Power is provided by a 5.3-liter V8 linked with a ten-speed automatic transmission and a two-speed transfer case. Acquired by the selling dealer in July 2024 and previously offered on BaT in July 2025 , this GMC Sierra has 29k miles and is now offered again in Iowa with a GMC window sticker, a Tuscany Motors build sheet, a damage-free Carfax report, and a clean Kentucky title.

The truck is finished in Onyx Black, and Official Harley-Davidson features include a revised front grille, hood, fender vents, and bumpers as well as Harley-Davidson badging, an LED light bar, a front skid plate, a carpeted bed liner, body-color fender flares, and dual billet exhaust tips with orange accents. Additional equipment includes a sunroof, a power-sliding rear window, power-retractable AMP Research running boards, a 5’8″ bed, and LED headlights with automatic high beams.

The 22” Harley-Davidson wheels are mounted with 35×12.50” Toyo Open Country tires. The truck features a lifted suspension with BDS components and Fox rear shock absorbers. Braking is handled by a disc at each corner.

The heated and ventilated front seats are upholstered in black leather with orange stitching and Harley-Davidson logos on the backrests, the rear bench is trimmed to match, and the cabin features a Harley-Davidson-branded center console, door-sill plates, and pedals. Factory equipment includes remote start, keyless entry, automatic climate control, a Bose sound system, and a touchscreen infotainment system with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto connectivity.

An emblem affixed to the center-console lid designates this truck as #0028 for the 2022 model year. 250 examples are said to have been produced.

The leather-wrapped steering wheel frames a digital display flanked by a Harley-Davidson-branded 140-mph speedometer and 6k-rpm tachometer. The digital odometer shows 29k miles, approximately 2k of which have been added by the selling dealer.

The 5.3-liter EcoTec3 V8 was factory rated at 355 horsepower and 383 lb-ft of torque. Power is sent to the rear or all four wheels through a ten-speed automatic transmission, a two-speed Autotrac transfer case, and a locking rear differential.
